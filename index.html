The Reserve Collection

The Craft Ritual

From Seed to Transcendence

01
Terroir Discovery

High-Altitude Sourcing

We sample hundreds of micro-lot harvests above 1,800m in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Panama, selecting only beans with extraordinary acidity and complex aromatics.

02
Hand Selection

Selective Harvesting

Only 100% ripe red cherries are hand-picked at peak Brix sweetness by master harvesters across volcanic soil slopes.

03
Fermentation

Anaerobic Ferment

Extended 96-hour anaerobic fermentation in sealed stainless vats on raised African beds unlocks vivid berry sweetness and jasmine aromatics.

04
Artisanal Roasting

Thermal Curve

Roasted in micro-batches on a cast-iron Loring roaster, tuned across 40 profile iterations to preserve delicate origin notes.

05
Maturation

Degassing & Rest

Beans are rested for 7 to 10 days in nitrogen-sealed flushed valve bags to allow flavor aromatics to blossom into full harmony.

06
Extraction

Sensory Extraction

Brewed with custom mineral water chemistry (120ppm) at 93.5°C to achieve crystal-clear sweetness, silky body, and infinite finish.
