Our 6-Step Ritual

01
Origin & Terroir

Finding the Perfect Farm

We travel to volcanic highlands and cloud-forest micro-lots, hand-selecting farms where altitude, rainfall, and soil converge into something extraordinary.

02
Selective Harvest

Hand-Picked at Peak Ripeness

Only fully ripe cherries are selected — a single pass yields one perfect cherry per branch, ensuring homogeneous sugar content and exceptional sweetness.

03
Processing Method

Washed, Honey & Natural

Each micro-lot is processed using the method that best reveals its terroir — whether washed clarity, honey sweetness, or the fruit-forward depth of natural drying.

04
Quality Control

Green Bean Sorting

Every batch is hand-sorted and optically scanned to remove any defects. Only beans scoring 85+ on the SCA scale proceed to the roastery.

05
Artisan Roasting

Small-Batch Precision Roasting

Our head roaster applies decade-refined profiles — controlled Maillard reactions and precise first-crack timing — to unlock every layer of complexity locked within the bean.

06
Cup & Dispatch

The Final Ritual

Each roast is cupped blind, scored, and dispatched within 48 hours in nitrogen-flushed, light-blocking pouches — arriving at your door at peak flavour.
